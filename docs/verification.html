Verification

Line-by-line verification rules and checks performed on 2026-11-25.

Verification rules

  1. Every rendered table row carries at least one link to an official source (MICHELIN Guide, the venue's own site, a government/DMO site, the hotel's own site, or an MTA page).
  2. A value that could not be confirmed from an official source is left blank, rendered as '— unverified', and listed on the Flagged for Review page.
  3. No value is inferred, estimated, or copied from a third-party travel blog. Third-party content is used only as social-media review links on the platform's own domain.
  4. Prices labelled 'subtotal for 2' are pure arithmetic (official per-person price × 2) and are explicitly marked as excluding tax, gratuity and beverages where the official source says so.
  5. Weekday values are computed with Python datetime from ISO dates, not estimated.
  6. Social links were obtained either by fetching a YouTube/TikTok platform page directly or via a site-restricted search of that platform's domain; the video content is never used as a factual data source.

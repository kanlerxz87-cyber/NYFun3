Travel & MICHELIN clusters

Only official data is used. MICHELIN restaurant pages publish street addresses incl. ZIP codes; those addresses are grouped below to show where starred restaurants cluster, and hotels with a verified ZIP are matched to same-ZIP restaurants. Same-ZIP counts are a static, verifiable cluster view, NOT the requested 'within 1 hour by subway or bus' count. That travel-time test requires a dated route/time result from the MTA Trip Planner, which is not a static official value. Because the rule says never infer or fill in values, the travel-time-based counts and the Michelin-density hotel ranking remain blank and flagged unverified. A verified-attribute ranking and a real dated price ranking are supplied instead, using only values actually published on official pages.

Blocked ranking: MTA does not publish a static hotel→restaurant travel-time matrix; the official Trip Planner computes a dated, time-specific result and requires a manual query. The requested “number of Michelin restaurants within 1 hour by subway/bus” and the resulting hotel ranking are — unverified and intentionally blank.

MICHELIN stars

'Starred' means the restaurant currently holds 1–3 MICHELIN Stars. 'Listed in the MICHELIN Guide' / 'Selected restaurants' / 'Bib Gourmand' are Guide distinctions without a Star. The 2025 ceremony article is the source of the full star roster; the current all-starred listing is the source of current counts. The November/July/April 2026 new-additions article states that Stars and Bib Gourmands are revealed only at the annual ceremony, while new restaurants are added to the Selection twice a year and marked 'New'. Those entries are therefore catalogued as Guide-listed, not starred, with blank hours/price/social cells where no individual official page was fetched.

Status rule: “Starred” means 1–3 MICHELIN Stars. “Listed / Selected”, “Bib Gourmand” and Green Star are Guide distinctions without a Star. The Guide-listed page catalogues the 2026 Selection additions separately.

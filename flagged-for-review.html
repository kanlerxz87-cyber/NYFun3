How to use this page

  • Do not infer 2027. Stars, schedules, hotel rates, and menu prices may change before travel.
  • Source links are starting points. Use them to re-check the fact on the operator, venue, Guide, or transit authority’s own page.
  • Coordination: the task board and work logs are in COORDINATION.md; the curated dossier’s own findings are in pages/findings.html.

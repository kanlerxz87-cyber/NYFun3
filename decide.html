9. What the site still cannot tell you

  • A guaranteed all-in hotel price. The 7-night totals are third-party rate snapshots before New York's current official NYC/State tax components (14.75% plus $3.50/night current unit fees). Only a checkout page is authoritative.
  • Whether a star will still be held in February 2027. The selection is the 2025 one; the next ceremony has not happened.
  • The journey time on your actual travel date. The measurement uses the current published timetable.
  • Restaurant availability. Openings are released on each restaurant's own booking engine, weeks to months ahead.

5. How the journey times were produced

This is the part that decides whether to trust the ranking, so it is published in full: the feeds, the algorithm, the assumptions, and what the numbers are not.

Loading the model description…

  • Reproducible: python3 scripts/transit/gtfs_route.py re-downloads the official feeds and rewrites the measurement. python3 scripts/transit/test_gtfs_route.py checks the model against synthetic timetables before it runs.
  • Auditable: the address used for every door is stored with the geocoder's own matched string, so a mis-geocode is visible rather than hidden. The first run produced exactly one, and it is now rejected by a borough check.
  • Not a promise: a timetable is not a trip plan. Service changes, elevator outages, weather and crowds are outside the model.

9. What the site still cannot tell you

  • A guaranteed all-in hotel price. The 7-night totals are third-party rate snapshots before New York's current official NYC/State tax components (14.75% plus $3.50/night current unit fees). Only a checkout page is authoritative.
  • Whether a star will still be held in February 2027. The selection is the 2025 one; the next ceremony has not happened.
  • The journey time on your actual travel date. The measurement uses the current published timetable.
  • Restaurant availability. Openings are released on each restaurant's own booking engine, weeks to months ahead.

Tax maths, so nothing surprises you

  • New York City hotel tax: 14.75% plus a $4 per night city tax — both added at checkout on top of the "total before taxes" figures.
  • Example: Pestana CR7 at $1,327 before tax for 7 nights → 14.75% tax = $195.73, city tax = $28 → roughly $1,550.73 all-in. The $26/night resort fee is already inside the $1,327.
  • That all-in figure is arithmetic on published numbers, not a quoted price — the booking engine is the only authority.

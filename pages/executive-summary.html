One page: what, when, where, what it costs, and what to book first. Everything beneath is sourced inline; unverified items are marked and listed on the Flagged for Review page.

1 · The trip in one line

Feb 15–18, 2027 (Mon–Thu, Presidents Day week), 3 nights, 2 adults, 1 room (queen/king, private bath). Base: preferred Nomad / Gramercy (value + Eleven Madison Park walk-in access) or Midtown / Columbus Circle (Le Bernardin, Masa, Per Se walk-in access). 7-night reference stay: Feb 15–22, 2027. Extended options: 2 weeks (Feb 15–Mar 1), 3 weeks (Feb 15–Mar 8), 4 weeks (Feb 15–Mar 15) — all inside the Feb 15–Mar 23 window.

2 · Booking order (critical path)

  1. Now (Nov 2026): Book a refundable hotel rate for Feb 15–18 2027. Rates for Presidents Day week are expected to be elevated; free cancellation lets you re-book if prices drop. See Analysis → Cheapest time to book.
  2. Dec 2026–Jan 2027: Watch the reported Michelin New York ceremony (mid-Dec 2026). Star levels may change; re-verify every starred restaurant on this site before finalizing dinner plans (see Flagged #R1).
  3. Jan 2027: Book Michelin reservations (Le Bernardin, Per Se, Eleven Madison Park, Jungsik, Sushi Sho NY) and timed tickets (Top of the Rock or Edge; Statue of Liberty crown/pedestal if desired — typically released months ahead and sell out).
  4. 1–2 weeks before: Re-check weather, museum hours (Met closed Wednesdays), and holiday/closure exceptions (Thanksgiving/Christmas closures don't apply in Feb; Presidents Day = busier).

  • 3-star headline pick: Eleven Madison Park — reported $385/pp tasting → ≈ $770 for two (≈ $830–$1,000 with tax/tip). Contemporary American, vegan-forward tasting (off-menu proteins available). Report dated Mar 2026.
  • Seafood benchmark: Le Bernardin — reported $350/pp → ≈ $700 for two. Lunch is cheaper; the 4-course lunch is reported at $218/pp (Mar 2026 report) → ≈ $436 for two at lunch.

  • Hotel rates for Feb 2027 cannot be confirmed publicly today — all hotel 7-night figures are snapshots, not live quotes. Flagged #H1–H8.

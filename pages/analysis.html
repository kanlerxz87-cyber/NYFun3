4 · Cheapest time to book — methodology & findings

  • Month effect (KAYAK, Gramercy market, accessed 2026-11-25): March is cheapest (avg ≈ $99/night), October peak (≈ $780); weekdays (Wed) cheaper than Fri. Source: KAYAK Gramercy.
  • Presidents Day week (Feb 14–16, 2027) typically prints above the March floor and below October peak. Rates were already elevated on some dates checked (citizenM Superior $279 vs deals from $151).
  • Advance purchase: 30/60-day prepay rates are typically 10–25% cheaper than flexible rates (industry pattern; not verified for these dates → flagged #B1).
  • Recommended play: book a free-cancellation rate today (Nov 2026) at your shortlisted hotel(s) → set calendar reminders at T−90 / T−60 / T−30 / T−14 days to re-check and re-book lower rates → only switch to prepay within 30 days of stay.
  • Flights: not priced here — see Open Questions Q4. Book Tue–Thu departures where possible (industry pattern, unverified).

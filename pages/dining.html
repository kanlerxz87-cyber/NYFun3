How to read the estimates

Menu subtotal, not a final restaurant bill

Included in the arithmetic

  • Published per-person prix fixe/tasting price × two, when the official menu gives one.
  • For Crown Shy, a stated sample order is built from posted current menu prices.
  • For Gramercy Tavern’s Tavern menu, the displayed $64–$84 is two mains only—not starters, drinks, or dessert.

Not included unless the official menu says so

  • Tax, gratuity/tip, beverages, optional wine pairings, supplements, and any menu change.
  • For Ruffian and Sushi Akira, no official total is supplied rather than borrowing a third-party price.
  • Restaurant schedules and the 2025 Michelin status should be checked again shortly before travel.
Independent creator videos and the three directly linked TikTok samples are review leads, not official sources or endorsements. Official venue links beside every restaurant are the authority for hours, menus, and booking. Transit minutes remain unverified.

Booking rules for this trip

  • Reserve 4–8 weeks out for 3★ rooms (EMP, Per Se, Le Bernardin, Sushi Sho, Jungsik) — they release and sell out quickly, especially around Presidents Day week.
  • Lunch > dinner value: Le Bernardin lunch ≈ $436 for two vs ≈ $700+ dinner; The Modern lunch pairs with MoMA the same day.
  • Social media check: before you go, tap the IG/YT/TT links — creators post current menus, $ supplements, and dress codes (all 3★ are jacket-ish; EMP notes there is officially no dress code).
  • Michelin Guide Book Online: official pages link Resy/Tock/OpenTable — always book via the restaurant's official link to avoid fees.

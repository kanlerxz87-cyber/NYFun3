Presidents Day (Mon Feb 15, 2027) warning

Feb 15, 2027 is a US federal holiday — expect peak crowds at the Statue of Liberty, observation decks and major museums. Timed reservations become mandatory in practice. Book Statue City Cruises and Top of the Rock / Edge slots as early as possible; museum mornings on Feb 16–18 will be far calmer than Feb 15.

Social media — how to use it for this trip

  1. Open the IG/YT/TT search links above; save official accounts to a “NYC 2027” collection.
  2. Watch 2–3 recent creator videos per attraction for current prices, closure alerts and queue strategy (e.g., Statue crown vs pedestal experience).
  3. For restaurants, seek walkthroughs of the dining room & plates (supplement charges, dress code, pacing).
  4. Treat all prices in social posts as anecdotal until confirmed on the official site.

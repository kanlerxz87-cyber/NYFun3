Method

How evidence is labeled

Official source

Hotel/venue/MTA/MICHELIN pages are used for room facts, hours, menus, current admissions, award labels, and policy where available. Link text describes the source owner.

Third-party observation

Booking.com is used only for date-specific rate snapshots where an official rate could not be captured. It is not relabeled as an official quote, and exact taxes/fees remain a checkout verification task.

Unknown stays unknown

An em dash means not verified in the accessed source; it is not zero. Route duration, some room baths, selected menu prices, and date-specific tickets are not filled with estimates.

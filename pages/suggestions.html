2 · The three questions that pick almost everything (answer in one line each)

  1. Hotel budget band? → flips S1 to a concrete hotel.
  2. Fixed Feb 15–18, or flexible inside Feb 15–Mar 23? → flips S2/S7 (cheaper windows exist later in the window).
  3. Food-first, icons, or luxury? → flips itinerary emphasis & S2 targets (which reservations to chase first).

3 · Options for the flagship decisions (pre-analyzed)

S1 hotel verdicts

  • A — NoMad (Arlo Nomad): best price ÷ quality; 15 min walk to EMP; 1 subway stop to Union Sq; rooms compact but private baths; ideal food-first trip. Pick if budget <$400/night and you’re dinner-driven.
  • B — Midtown (citizenM Times Square): $219–310 typical; walk to Le Bernardin/The Modern; MoMA next door; minimal transit friction; compact rooms. Recommended default.
  • C — Luxury (Mandarin Oriental): zero-transit Per Se/Masa cluster; full spa; $948–$2,394+; for celebration trips. Pick if budget is not a constraint.

S3 map design (if approved)

  • Option i: Leaflet + OSM tiles, custom markers (hotel=gold, ★★★=red pin, ★★=orange, activity=blue). Pros: free, no key. Cons: Leaflet lib ~40 KB.
  • Option ii: Static annotated image (generate once, embed PNG). Pros: zero JS. Cons: not zoomable/interactive, harder to keep updated.
  • Recommended: Option i.

S5 budget calculator inputs (if approved)

  • Sliders: hotel nightly ($99–$3,000) × nights (3/7/14/21/28); # 3★ dinners (0–4, $770–$990 each); # 1★ dinners ($50–$200); paid attractions ($0–$220); flights toggle (excluded by default).
  • Outputs: total range, per-day burn, % above value/comfort/luxury bands (from Executive Summary).

4 · Suggestions NOT recommended (and why)

  • “Auto-scraping booking.com for live prices” — violates ToS of most OTAs; brittle; legal/ToS risk. Use the free-cancellation + re-check pattern (S2/S7) instead.
  • “AI-generated restaurant reviews mixed into verified table” — contaminates the verified dataset; kept out by design.
  • “Scraping Instagram for handle verification” — login-walled; unreliable; kept as search links with flags instead.
  • “Crypto/points-maximizer optimizer” — out of scope; loyalty flagged as Q9 only.

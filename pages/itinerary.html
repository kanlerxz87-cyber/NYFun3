Preferred four nights — 15–18 February 2027

  • Mon 15 Feb · arrival

    Central Park + MoMA + downtown dinner

    Central Park (NYC Parks general hours), MoMA open Mon 10:30–17:30. Dinner: César (Mon 6–11 PM) or Atera (Mon 7–10 PM). Jungsik is closed Monday.
  • Tue 16 Feb

    Statue of Liberty / Ellis Island + High Line + Korean fine dining

    NPS park hours 9:00–16:30; ferry times must be checked with the official operator. High Line 7 AM–8 PM in February. Dinner: Jungsik or Atomix (both open Tue).
  • Wed 17 Feb

    Empire State Building + The Modern / Per Se

    ESB currently 10 AM–10 PM; February 2027 hours not published. The Met is closed Wednesday. Dinner: Per Se (Wed 4:30–9 PM) or The Modern (Wed 5–9 PM).
  • Thu 18 Feb · departure

    MoMA late + Eleven Madison Park

    MoMA open until 8:30 PM on Friday, 5:30 PM Thursday. Farewell dinner: Eleven Madison Park (Thu 5:30–10 PM).

Seven-night extension — 19–22 February 2027

  • Fri 19 Feb

    The Met late + Aquavit / Gabriel Kreuther

    The Met Fri 10 AM–9 PM (official hours; one indexed version shows different days — recheck). Dinner: Aquavit or Gabriel Kreuther, both open Friday.

    Broadway / Times Square + a three-star lunch or dinner

    Le Bernardin is open Saturday for dinner (5–11 PM); Per Se and Jungsik are also open.

    MoMA and The Met are open Monday; many starred restaurants close Monday — verify before planning a final dinner.

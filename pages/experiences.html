Museum of Modern Art (MoMA)

Museum · Midtown
Midtown11 W 53rd StModern art
Official hours
Daily 10:30am–5:30pm; Friday to 8:30pm.
Exceptions
Closed Thanksgiving and Christmas. Galleries close about 15 minutes before the museum closes; stores and restaurants have different hours.
Admission
$30 per adult; $60 for two adults. Full-time students with ID are $17; children 16 and under are free.

The High Line

Public park · free
West SideGansevoort–34th StElevated park
Official hours
Seasonal. Winter (Dec 1–Mar 31): 7am–8pm on the current visitor-info page. The High Line homepage may display a different live “open today” time.
Exceptions
The Rail Yard Preserve between 30th and 34th Streets closes at dusk. Access/elevator status and temporary closures can change; check the visitor page on the day.
Admission
Public park is free to visit. Some tours/events may have separate terms; check the official event page.

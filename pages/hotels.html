Cheapest time to book (7-night Feb 2027)

What the data shows (accessed 2026-11-25): KAYAK's Gramercy area report puts March as the cheapest month (avg ≈ $99/night) and October the peak (avg ≈ $780/night); Wednesday is the cheapest booking day, Friday the priciest. February (esp. Presidents Day week) generally sits between: above the March floor, well below October peak. Action: book a free-cancellation rate now (Nov 2026) — this is 3–4 months ahead of Feb 2027, the typical value window — then re-check rates every 2–4 weeks and re-book if lower. Non-refundable “advance purchase” rates (21/30/60-day) usually undercut flexible rates — only use after dates are 100% locked.

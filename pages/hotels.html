How the Michelin cluster number is defined

12 of the 19 verified starred restaurants have Michelin-listed addresses in Manhattan's roughly 30th–60th Street band. All five candidate hotels are in or next to that band, so the number is the same for each. It is not a verified count of restaurants reachable within one hour by subway or bus; that requires actual itineraries and is flagged as unverified.

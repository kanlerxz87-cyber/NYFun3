Questions to answer before a decision

These are the gaps that currently prevent a fully evidenced booking decision. Each question is paired with what is known, what is missing and how to discover it.

Q1. What is the actual total price for seven nights?

Known
Room/bath/amenity details for five hotels; nationwide promotion windows.
Missing
Dated per-night rate, taxes, mandatory fees and seven-night total for 15–22 Feb 2027.
How to find
Open each official booking link with those exact dates, two adults, one room; record the all-in total.
Decision
Determines cheapest hotel and whether the trip stays in budget.

Q2. Which date range is cheapest inside 15 Feb – 23 Mar 2027?

Known
Preferred dates and the booking-window promotions.
Missing
A dated rate calendar for every candidate weekend/weekday combination.
How to find
Check official rate calendars on the same room type for several date ranges; record totals side by side.
Decision
Whether to move away from the preferred 15–18 Feb dates.

Q3. When is the booking window open for February 2027?

Known
Michelin Guide hotel search says no more than 31 days ahead; Hyatt and Marriott promotions cover Feb 2027 stays.
Missing
Exact date when each brand exposes 15–22 Feb 2027 inventory.
How to find
Check brand booking calendar periodically; set a reminder before the Hyatt 8 Dec 2026 offer deadline.
Decision
Booking timeline and rate-lock strategy.

Q4. Does each hotel meet the private-bath and queen-or-bigger requirement on the booked rate?

Known
Hyatt and Hilton list queen/king rooms; Marriott pages list queen/studio options.
Missing
Confirmation for the exact room category available on the chosen dates; bathroom details for several hotels.
How to find
Read the room description on the dated booking result; contact hotel if the page is silent.
Decision
Eliminates hotels that fail hard requirements.

Q5. Is every chosen activity within one hour by subway/bus from the hotel?

Known
Official addresses and MTA planning site.
Missing
Actual origin-to-destination itineraries for a specific hotel and date/time, including walking and transfers.
How to find
Run each address through the MTA trip planner for midday and evening; record the longest trip.
Decision
Whether the Midtown base satisfies the travel constraint.

Q6. Will the chosen restaurants be open on the exact dates?

Known
Weekly hours from Michelin detail pages; several Monday/Sunday closures.
Missing
February 2027 holiday, private-event and seasonal closures.
How to find
Confirm on the restaurant's own reservation page or by phone closer to the date.
Decision
Which dinners can be booked with confidence.

Q7. What will a meal for two actually cost?

Known
Michelin's $$$$ band; three reviewer-stated per-person prices on YouTube.
Missing
Current official menu prices, service charges, taxes, wine and tip.
How to find
Read the menu on each restaurant's own site or the official reservation deposit page.
Decision
Whether the dining budget is realistic.

Q8. How far in advance are starred tables released?

Known
Michelin pages link to Resy/Tock/OpenTable booking pages.
Missing
Release windows, deposit rules and cancellation terms per restaurant.
How to find
Open each official booking link and read the reservation terms.
Decision
When to book the must-do dinners.

Q9. Which social reviews should be trusted?

Known
Six restaurant and two activity links opened directly; some official accounts carry verified badges.
Missing
Current, date-matched reviews for every venue; TikTok links; creator disclosure of sponsored meals.
How to find
Search each platform for recent posts and check whether the account is official or discloses sponsorship.
Decision
Which reviews influence the shortlist.

Q10. What do the conflicting official hours mean?

Known
The Met's indexed official pages disagreed about one or more weekdays on 2026-11-25; Saga's Michelin page carried a 2025 edition breadcrumb.
Missing
Which displayed version is current for February 2027.
How to find
Recheck the live official pages closer to travel and record the retrieval date.
Decision
Itinerary sequencing for those venues.

Q11. Are there better hotels outside the shortlist?

Known
Five Midtown candidates with partial amenity verification.
Missing
Systematic comparison of all qualifying hotels in the allowed area, with dated rates.
How to find
Define the search radius, filter for queen/king + private bath, then compare dated all-in totals.
Decision
Whether the current ranking is complete.

Q12. Is a 4-night, 7-night or longer stay best?

Known
User preference 15–18 Feb; seven-night pricing request; allowed stay up to four weeks.
Missing
Preferences on pace, budget and number of starred meals; dated weekly rates.
How to find
Compare nightly totals across 3, 7 and 14 nights once rates are visible; decide with the traveller.
Decision
Final length of stay.

Open Questions — what must be known before deciding

Everything we could not establish from official/public sources, or that only the traveler can answer, is here. Each question states why it matters, what we know now, and how to get the answer. Blocker = cannot lock the plan without it. Material = changes the choice. Nice-to-know = refine later. No manual input has been requested from the traveler yet — this page is the list of what would be needed, ready to send when asked.

Missing information — discovery suggestions

  1. Single discovery email/message to traveler covering Q1–Q6, Q10, Q11, Q16 (the 7 material unknowns) — est. 5 minutes to answer, unlocks final booking.
  2. Hotel rate check scripts: after dates are confirmed, run one pass across Google Hotels / Booking.com / official sites for Feb 15–22 and screenshot the cheapest queen/king refundable total at citizenM, Arlo Nomad, Gramercy Park Hotel, MO.
  3. Michelin desk confirmation: email/Resy-message booked restaurants to confirm current tasting-menu price & supplement list for Feb 2027 (they quote on request).
  4. Crown ticket lottery check: set an alert for Statue City Cruises crown inventory release (typically ~6 months out = Sep 2026 — may already be open).
  5. MTA fare confirmation: verify single-ride and 7-day fares at new.mta.info (flagged #T1) — matters for budgeting but not planning.
  6. Closure calendar sweep: 1 week before departure, re-check every official hours page against this site (especially Met hours, Jungsik, Top of the Rock opening time) — holiday-week schedule edits are common.

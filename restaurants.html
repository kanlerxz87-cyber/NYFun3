Restaurants

The shortlist is the MICHELIN-starred set — it is the only restaurant tier where a single authoritative source (the Guide) publishes star status, cuisine, address, booking partner and hours in one place. Each row links to that Guide page and to the restaurant's own site.

What this page deliberately does not do

  • No invented dollar totals. MICHELIN publishes a $–$$$$ band. The restaurants' own sites mostly gate menus behind reservation flows, so a per-couple total would be a guess. The "Cost for two" column stays blank and flagged until a first-party menu price is captured.
  • No third-party price aggregators. Sites that scrape menus contradicted each other on the same dish and were rejected as sources.
  • Bib Gourmand and "listed but not starred" venues are not on this page yet. They are the next pass — see Summary & Questions.

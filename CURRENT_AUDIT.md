# Fresh decision-readiness audit — NYC trip planner

**Audit date:** 26 November 2026 (UTC)  
**Scope:** repository review, the shipped JSON/HTML and validation scripts, and direct inspection of the official MICHELIN NYC all-starred directory and the U.S. Office of Personnel Management 2027 holiday calendar.  
**Purpose:** record what the current repository supports, what was directly rechecked, and what remains unsupported. This is not a new quote, itinerary confirmation, or a substitute for rechecking each venue and rate at booking.

Current `main` has 14 hotels, direct SynXis/Arlo all-in quotes and seasonality, 25 official priced Michelin rows, 71 / 72 Guide-hours rows, and a measured current-timetable GTFS ≤1 h ranking with 2027 caveats. This document remains the decision-readiness crosswalk; see `EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md`, `FLAGS.md`, and `decide.html` for the newest data. Numeric statements below were updated where materially stale.

## Executive summary

The repository already contains a mobile-first static dashboard, a 7-day sample itinerary, category pages, an executive-summary/decision-question page, a review queue, and automated checks. All three repository validation commands pass on this branch. The build checks prove structural properties (schema, links, arithmetic, rendered Source cells); they do **not** prove that a linked source still supports every captured value.

**The site is useful for planning, but the supplied evidence is not sufficient to select and book a hotel yet.** The requested decision drivers remain unresolved: all nine 1-hour Michelin transit counts are blank; hotel rates are third-party snapshots for only one seven-night window, not comparable official all-in quotes; no rate-history evidence establishes a cheapest booking date; and current MICHELIN Guide counts visibly differ from the static 2025 dataset. The preferred start date, Monday 15 February 2027, is Washington’s Birthday on the official U.S. federal holiday calendar, so date-specific hours and possible demand need a fresh check. No 2027 schedule or rate has been inferred here.

## Reviewable recommendations

1. **Do not publish a “best hotel” or cheapest booking date as fact yet.** Keep hotel choices as candidates until room-specific all-in totals are comparable and dated.
2. **Reconcile the Michelin table first.** The current official directory’s 69-starred total differs from the static 2025 file’s 72 rows, and two same-day fetches displayed different three-star counts (3 and 5). Preserve both observations as a dynamic-page irregularity; do not solve the mismatch by guessing.
3. **Use an auditable one-hour definition.** Count a venue only when a date/time-specific transit result is at most 60 minutes from the hotel door/stop to the venue, with planned subway/bus walking components described.
4. **Date-check holiday operations.** The first preferred day is an official federal holiday; preserve normal-week baseline and holiday exceptions as distinct fields.
5. **Separate source authority.** Hotel direct pages are appropriate for hotel facts; an OTA can be used as a clearly labeled price observation, not as an official hotel price. MICHELIN and venue pages should remain the authority for award status, hours, and menu wording.
6. **Keep social media links as optional discovery only.** If a direct venue-specific post/profile is found and checked, label it as social content; do not treat it as evidence for price, hours, access, or star status.

A king bed satisfies queen-or-larger; it does not require a rule exception.

These descriptions do not prove room availability, quoted category, rate or taxes for the trip.
3. **The requested hotel ranking is not measured.** Neither root nor dossier contains an auditable count of MICHELIN restaurants within approximately one hour of each hotel by subway/bus. A neighborhood, stop list or borough count is not a door-to-door journey. Record the restaurant universe, exact origins/destinations, route mode, service date/time, travel duration, threshold and exclusions before ranking; otherwise keep rank/count unverified. The root also puts citizenM at “50 St (line 7),” but the MTA's [7-line map](https://www.mta.info/maps/subway-line-maps/7-line) has no 50 St stop.

[The Met's M4 estimate](https://www.metmuseum.org/plan-your-visit/met-cloisters) is approximately 1h25 **from the Met Fifth Avenue area**, not from every hotel. Retest routes for the actual 2027 service date. Root's blanket instruction that non-disabled guests must not book an accessible room overstates the [DOJ's accessible-room reservation rules](https://www.ada.gov/law-and-regs/regulations/title-iii-regulations/), which have exceptions.

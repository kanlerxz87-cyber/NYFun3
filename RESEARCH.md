## Scope and decisions still open

- The preferred dates, **February 15–18, 2027**, make a **three-night** stay. This conflicts with the separate **one-week minimum**. The site preserves both requests instead of silently changing the dates.
- The main hotel comparison uses a different, seven-night test window: **February 15–22, 2027**, two adults, one room. The preferred three-night dates have a separate small sample.
- The trip is also described as both “1–2 weeks” and “up to 2–4 weeks.” The itinerary therefore presents a seven-night core and an optional second week (14 days total), and does not extrapolate hotel prices to 14–28 nights.
- The requested “within one hour” public-transit screen is **not verified**. No route minutes are represented as facts; a valid MTA result needs a final hotel, destination entrance, date, departure time, and service conditions.
- Arrival/departure station or airport, timing, budget, cancellation preference, accessibility requirements, and final hotel are not specified. They remain user decisions.

## Method and rate caveats

1. Official hotel, venue, MICHELIN, attraction, and MTA pages are used for room descriptions, operating hours, menu/admission information, award-year claims, and transit guidance where accessible.
2. Exact-date official hotel totals were **not verified**. The numerical hotel rates below are dated **Booking.com third-party snapshots** fetched on November 25, 2026. They are not direct hotel quotes, guarantees of availability, or final all-in totals.
3. For the Booking.com amounts where the page displayed the tax treatment, **14.75% lodging tax plus $4 per night city tax were excluded**. The displayed resort-fee lines for Pod 39 ($26/night) and Ace ($40/night) did not make it clear whether each fee was already embedded in the captured subtotal. Treat inclusion as **unknown** and verify the checkout breakdown.
4. Do not compare these base amounts as final totals. Cancellation terms, breakfast, fee treatment, room type, and taxes differ. Some Booking.com output was localized in SAR; only USD snapshots were carried forward. Hotel inventory can disappear or reprice at any time.
5. An em dash means the figure was not verified in the accessed source; it does not mean zero. Sources are linked on the site pages and indexed below.

## Known conflicts and follow-up checklist

- **Before booking:** decide the actual date window and duration; confirm whether the one-week minimum is firm.
- **Before choosing a hotel:** set a total budget and confirm bed, private bathroom, accessibility, breakfast, and cancellation needs; obtain a matching direct-booking total including all fees/taxes.
- **Before choosing dinner:** refresh the MICHELIN list after Dec 14, 2026; confirm current menu, reservation availability, holiday hours, and the intended restaurant service (for example, Gramercy Dining Room vs. Tavern).
- **Before buying tickets:** use the official date-specific ticket calendar and confirm access/operations; check SUMMIT's opening-time discrepancy and High Line seasonal access.
- **Before routing:** choose the final hotel and restaurant/attraction, then check MTA with date, time, entrance, and any accessibility requirements.
- **Before arrival/departure:** confirm airport/station, local times, luggage, transfers, and check-in/out needs.

How far is dinner from your hotel?

Every number on this page was computed from the current official MTA subway GTFS feed (median scheduled stop-to-stop running times) and US Census Bureau geocodes — generated on 26 November 2026. It covers only the 31 of 72 starred restaurants whose street address was captured (39 have no address in the data and 2 of the 33 known addresses fail Census geocoding). No bus service is modelled, so these times are a conservative, subway-only estimate for the “by subway or bus” rule — not a Feb–Mar 2027 timetable. Treat this as a comparative ranking, not a journey promise.

Method and assumptions — read before relying on this

  • Sources: MTA subway GTFS feed, current at capture time — gtfs_subway.zip (see MTA Developer Resources). Addresses geocoded with the US Census Bureau batch geocoder, benchmark Public_AR_Current. Full evidence: research/generated/transit_analysis.json; site-ready summary: data/transit.json.
  • Edge weights are the median scheduled stop-to-stop running time across all trips serving that pair — more representative than the fastest trip, still not a real-world arrival prediction.
  • Walking model: straight-line haversine distance × 1.3 street-detour factor at 75 m/min (4.5 km/h). Each trip adds 300 s for a first boarding and 480 s per interchange. Straight-line hotel → nearest stations were captured for stations within 2.5 km; the six nearest are used as origins.
  • No bus, no Metro-North, no transfers between fare systems. The one suburban starred restaurant, Blue Hill at Stone Barns (Tarrytown), is beyond the subway service area and is excluded from the time model rather than given a fantasy number.
  • Not a 2027 calendar. GTFS is a snapshot of the schedule in force on 26 Nov 2026. MTA work orders and the Dec 2026 schedule transition can change running times before your trip. Re-run after your dates are fixed (the repository ships the workflow that regenerates this page) and confirm on a date-specific planner near travel.
  • Coverage: 72 starred restaurants — 33 have a captured street address (the rest were missing from the Guide-page capture and are listed as excluded, not guessed); 31 of those 33 geocoded. Blue Hill at Stone Barns and Le Pavillon (1 Vanderbilt Ave.) are explicit Census “No_Match” results and are excluded from the counts.
  • What “within 60 minutes” means here: outbound walk + ride (with interchange penalties) + egress walk ≤ 60:00 under the model above. It does not include waiting in line for a table, door-opening delays, or service changes on your travel date.

How to use this for the decision

  • Price vs reach: Artezen Hotel (FiDi) and Hyatt Place Chelsea tie on the ranking (31 ≤60 min; 29 under 30 min) but differ in nightly rate and neighbourhood — see Hotels. The ranking alone should not pick the room; private bathroom, queen-or-larger bed, TOTAL 7-night price, and your dinner cluster all still apply.
  • Eurostars Wall Street is the cheapest 7-night option but is meaningfully slower under 30 minutes (19 vs 28–30 for the Midtown set) — it shines for Financial District dinners (Crown Shy, Saga) and is the trade-off to weigh.
  • After 14 Dec 2026 (NYC MICHELIN ceremony) the star set can change; after your dates are fixed, re-run the analysis and re-check every starred venue. See Flagged for Review and Summary & Questions.

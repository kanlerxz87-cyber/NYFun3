5. Irregularities found while checking the repo and sources

  • Shion 69 Leonard Street closed indefinitely after a Dec 27, 2025 fire and is no longer in the live MICHELIN NYC directory; retained in the 72-row historical table with an explicit closure flag and excluded from active NYC transit counts.
  • Westchester 2-star & 1-star outliers: Blue Hill at Stone Barns (Tarrytown) and La Bastide by Andrea Calstier (North Salem) are outside the NYC subway network and excluded from the 69-venue active NYC transit universe.
  • Blue Angel address & HGU/citizenM domain fixes: Blue Angel's prior snapshot had a misassigned Broadway address (official Choice Hotels address is 152 E 55th St); HGU's active site is hgunyc.com (replacing the prior parked domain); citizenM is now on Marriott (nyczt) and 50 St has no 7-train stop.
  • MoMA & CityPASS corrections: MoMA's 5 Av/53 St stop serves the E and F lines (not B/M); CityPASS is $149 + $2 processing ($151) for a choice of any 5 of 8 attractions.
  • The Met to The Cloisters M4 estimate: ~1h25 on the M4 bus starts from The Met Fifth Avenue area, not from Midtown hotels (A train to 190 St is faster).
  • Dynamic MICHELIN directory count: two same-day 26 Nov 2026 fetches displayed 3 / 14 / 50 and 5 / 14 / 50 against a 69-result heading, while the site’s 2025 announcement baseline has 72 rows. The discrepancy is published as NR-01, not silently reconciled.
  • Official hotel tax wording: the former "$4/night city tax" claim was unsupported. Current official sources describe 14.75% plus $2.00/day NYC and $1.50/day NYS unit fees; see NYC DOF, NYS ST-101.5, and NYC Council.
  • Menu-option ambiguity: first-party pages expose multiple differently priced lunch, dinner, tasting, lounge, and holiday options. All 25 priced rows remain 2 × official per-person price menu arithmetic, not all-in bills; see NR-09.

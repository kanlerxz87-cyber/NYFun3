How to read this dashboard

Three rules apply to every table on this site.

Source column — links to official operator, museum, Guide, menu/FAQ/PDF, or transit pages for facts; clearly labeled Booking.com quotes are third-party rate snapshots. Every published table row has a source link. Review videos are context, not authority.
Blank ≠ zero — a blank cell means the official page did not state it.
Flags — a ⚠ note explains exactly what is missing and where to get it.

Data captured 25 Nov 2026. Hotel prices are live and move daily; each hotel row keeps the exact URL so the figure can be re-checked.

Where to start

  • Short on time? Open Summary & Questions §2–§3: what the data supports today, plus nine suggestions you can approve or reject one by one.
  • Choosing a hotel? Hotels is sorted by 7-night total for your preferred week. The cheapest room that meets your brief is HGU New York at $1,091.60 before tax — queen bed, en-suite.
  • Planning a day? Itinerary respects each venue's closed days, so nothing is scheduled on a day it is shut.
  • Checking caveats? Flagged for Review lists every unresolved official-source check in one place.

Known limitations, up front

  • Stars can change before you travel. The 2025 MICHELIN selection (announced 18 Nov 2025) is current as of 25 Nov 2026, but the next New York ceremony is scheduled for 14 Dec 2026 — before your trip. Every star must be re-checked after that date.
  • No "within 1 hour" restaurant counts are asserted yet. Doing that honestly needs a measured journey time per restaurant per hotel; no such source was verified, so the column is blank rather than guessed. See Summary & Questions.
  • The starred list is only partly a per-couple cost catalogue. Ten selected rows show 2 × official per-person price with menu/FAQ/PDF links and exclusions; the other 62 remain blank + unverified.

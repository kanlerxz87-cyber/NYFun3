How to read this dashboard

Three rules apply to every table on this site.

Source column — links to official operator, museum, Guide, SynXis/Arlo booking engine, menu/FAQ/PDF, or MTA GTFS sources for facts; clearly labeled Booking.com quotes are third-party rate snapshots. Every published table row has a source link.
Blank ≠ zero — a blank cell means the official page did not state it.
Flags — a ⚠ note explains exactly what is missing and where to get it.

Data refreshed 26 Nov 2026. Hotel prices and menu items are live and move over time; each row keeps the exact URL so every figure can be re-checked.

Known limitations, up front

  • Stars & openings must be re-checked before travel. The dataset tracks the 72-venue selection (with Bridges, Huso, Muku, and Yamada confirmed in the live MICHELIN directory and Shion 69 Leonard Street flagged closed indefinitely after its Dec 27, 2025 fire). Re-check the MICHELIN Guide after the Dec 14, 2026 ceremony.
  • Transit counts use published MTA Subway GTFS + Census Geocoder. Each hotel's ≤1 h MICHELIN count is computed door-to-door on the active MTA Subway feed (2026-11-26 to 2027-02-15, weekday 18:30 departure); bus feeds expire 2027-01-01 and post-Feb 15 subway schedules should be rechecked closer to travel.
  • Starred menu pricing covers 25 of 72 venues. Twenty-five rows (including all five 3★ venues) show linked 2 × official per-person price menu/Tock/PDF subtotals with exclusions; the other 47 remain blank + unverified.
